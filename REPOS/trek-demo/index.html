    Why small

    Altitude punishes crowds. We cap every departure at twelve

    Twenty-person tour groups walk at the pace of the fastest ego and split up when someone gets a headache. Our 1:3 guide ratio means an assistant guide can walk down with an acclimatising trekker without ending anyone else’s trip.

    Questions we answer every week

    How fit do I need to be?

    If you can hike 5–6 hours with a 6 kg daypack on consecutive weekends, you are ready for Langtang. Annapurna Sanctuary and Gokyo Ri reward eight weeks of stair or hill training before departure.

    What happens if my oxygen saturation drops?

    Guides log resting heart rate and SpO2 at breakfast and dinner above 3,000 m. If you show moderate AMS symptoms, we hold an extra acclimatisation night or descend immediately with a dedicated assistant guide.

    Can solo travellers join a group?

    Over half our bookings are solo trekkers. You share the trail with up to eleven others and have the option of your own private lodge room in lower villages.

    What is included in the trek fee?

    All TIMS and National Park permits, Kathmandu airport transfers, Lukla flights (for Gokyo), tea-house lodging, three hot meals + trail tea daily, licensed guides, insured porters, and emergency oxygen.
